sections:
  description:
    title: What it's about
    text: local.ch commissioned CoRelation to stage the phone book at the exhibition «Das begehbare Buch» in Zurich's Seefeld district. Under the motto «Helden des Alltags», the phone book was given a stage in its own room and offered insight into its 130-year history. The presence was supported by a broadly based online and print campaign. Together with our partner network, we implemented all measures — from concept to the complete solution — in just three weeks.
      
  challenge:
    title: Challenge  
    text: "Behind every phone book entry there is a person with a story. The goal was to make these everyday heroes visible and to tell their stories vividly. For this we developed a campaign that directly involved the audience at the Villa Mainau: visitors could enter their own stories via a specially developed online application and publish them on the accompanying website."

      
  result:
    title: Result
    text: The campaign combined exhibition, events and digital platforms into a unique experience. Celebrities from music and literature added further accents. A web application and website, flanked by print and online campaigns, a Facebook page as well as targeted media relations and a media conference, generated nationwide attention. Four films documented the exhibition and reinforced the impact lastingly.
